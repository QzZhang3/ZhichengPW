# About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at **The University of New South Wales (UNSW)** in Australia, where my research focuses on computer vision, generative models, and multimodal learning. Prior to joining UNSW, I received my Master's degree in Computer Science from **The University of Queensland (UQ)**, where I was awarded the **Dean's Commendation for Academic Excellence**.

My research centers on developing intelligent and generalizable multimodal AI systems, with particular interests in **diffusion models, neural decoding, audio-driven visual generation, and multimodal representation learning**. I am especially interested in bridging human perception and artificial intelligence by exploring how visual, auditory, and neural signals can be jointly modeled to enable more perceptually aligned and human-centered generative systems.

My research has resulted in publications at multiple **international academic conferences**, and I have been granted several **patents and software copyrights**.
